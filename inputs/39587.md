## Description
The channel card and group card had a few styling inconsistencies:

- The channel card had extra vertical space between the channel name and its description.

- The group name was larger and used a different color than the channel name.

- Group card icons (header + footer) were shifted up by 1px, while the channel card icon was not.

This PR aligns both cards so they look visually consistent.

For Group card compact members, instead of a scrolling member list, the card now names a couple of the
group's direct subgroups (by name) and members, and summarizes the rest as `and N more`. The description is clamped to two lines, and the footer is a gear "Settings" link.

For Channel card descriptions, the Long channel descriptions are clamped to two lines, a divider separates the subscriber count, and the footer link is simplified to "Settings".

When a description is actually truncated, hovering over it shows a tooltip pointing to settings for the full text (it doesn't appear for short descriptions)

Discussion: [CZO](https://chat.zulip.org/#narrow/channel/101-design/topic/channel.20cards.3F/near/2481778) discussion
