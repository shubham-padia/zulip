## Description
The "Go to direct messages with..." tool tip is not displayed any more in dedicated 1:1 and group DM tabs. Likewise, the "Go to #channel > topic" tool tip is hidden in dedicated topic tabs.
The conditions are applied only on the above cases. The "Go to #channel" tool tip is available in topic tabs, while DM/topic navigation tool tips keep functioning in other scenarios ealier.

### #40026 (issue, open): Suppress redundant 'Go to DM' and 'Go to topic' tooltips, when already in that location
Opened by emilysutherlin (MEMBER). Labels: area: popovers and tooltips, help wanted, in progress. Assignees: adityas27.
Description: We should suppress redundant tooltips in the header of DM view and topic view.

In 1-to-1 and group DMs, a tooltip appears on hover over the top recipient bar:"Go to direct messages with..." ( shown [here](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4e279ec0-bf58-481b-a45f-fbc9f4d50517))
In topic view, a similar tooltip reads "Go to #channel > topic" when hovering over the topic name.

In both of these situations, that's where you already are, so clicking has no effect. This is additionally confusing in the topics view, because it misrepresents the effect of the `S` shortcut.

CZO thread: [#feedback > redundant 'Go to DM' and 'Go to topic' tooltip](https://chat.zulip.org/#narrow/channel/137-feedback/topic/redundant.20.27Go.20to.20DM.27.20and.20.27Go.20to.20topic.27.20tooltip/with/2515448)

Note: we do not want to suppress the "Go to #channel" tooltip (or any other tooltips) in the topic view. And we do not want to suppress the 'Go to DM' or 'Go to topic' tooltips in any other locations, such as interleaved views, where they are correct.
